Given this extensive data mining, it does not make sense to use the usual criteria for
establishing signiﬁcance. Which hurdle should be used for current research? Our paper
introduces a new multiple testing framework and provides historical cutoffs from the ﬁrst
empirical tests in 1967 to today. A new factor needs to clear a much higher hurdle, with
a t-statistic greater than 3.0. We argue that most claimed research ﬁndings in ﬁnancial
economics are likely false. ( JEL C12, C52, G12)
