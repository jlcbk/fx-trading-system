We study a dynamic portfolio optimization problem related t o convergence
trading, which is an investment strategy that exploits temp orary mispricing by simultane-
ously buying relatively underpriced assets and selling sho rt relatively overpriced ones with
the expectation that their prices converge in the future. We build on the model of Liu and
Timmermann [22] and extend it by incorporating unobservabl e Markov-modulated pricing
errors into the price dynamics of two co-integrated assets. We characterize the optimal
portfolio strategies in full and partial information setti ngs both under the assumption of
unrestricted and beta-neutral strategies. By using the inn ovations approach, we provide
the ﬁltering equation that is essential for solving the opti mization problem under partial
information. Finally, in order to illustrate the model capa bilities, we provide an example
with a two-state Markov chain.
