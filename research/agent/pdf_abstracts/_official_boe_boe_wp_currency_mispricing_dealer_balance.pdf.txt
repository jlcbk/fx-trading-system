We relate currency mispricing originating from the breakdown of covered interest rate parity to the dealer 
balance-sheet constraints resulting from the post-crisis financial regulation. Using a unique data set on 
contract-level foreign exchange derivatives with disclosed counterparty identities, we find that dealers 
with a higher leverage ratio demand an additional premium from their clients for synthetic dollar funding. 
We handle endogeneity using two exogenous variations associated with the public disclosure of the 
leverage ratio, and the introduction of the UK leverage ratio framework while controlling for changes in 
demand conditions at the client level.
Key words: Exchange rates, dollar basis covered interest parity condition, arbitrage opportunities.
