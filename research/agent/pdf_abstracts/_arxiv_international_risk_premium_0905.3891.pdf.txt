In this article, we investigate whether exchange ra te risk is priced. We use a multivariate GARCH-in-
Mean specification and test alternative conditional international CAPM versions. Our results support 
strongly the international asset-pricing model that in cludes exchange rate risk for both developed and 
emerging stock markets. However, there are important time and cross-country variations in the relative 
size and dynamics of different risk premia.
