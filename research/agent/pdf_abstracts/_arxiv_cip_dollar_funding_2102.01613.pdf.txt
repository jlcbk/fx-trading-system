A palindromic composition of n is a composition of n which can
be read the same way forwards and backwards. In this paper we d eﬁne an
anti-palindromic composition of n to be a composition of n which has no
mirror symmetry amongst its parts. W e then give a surprising connection
between the number of anti-palindromic compositions of n and the so-called
tribonacci sequence, a generalization of the Fibonacci seq uence. W e conclude
by deﬁning a new q-analogue of the Fibonacci sequence, which is related to
certain equivalence classes of anti-palindromic composit ions.
