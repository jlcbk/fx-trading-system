Summary
In this article, we derive and compare methods to derivep-values and sets of con-
ﬁdence intervals with strong control of the family-wise error rates and coverage for
estimates of treatment eﬀects in cluster randomised trials with multiple outcomes.
Therearefewmethodsfor p-valuecorrectionsandderivingconﬁdenceintervals,lim-
iting their application in this setting. We discuss the methods of Bonferroni, Holm,
and Romano & Wolf (2005) and adapt them to cluster randomised trial inference
